Would this have made money?

Flat $100 a bet, standard -110 vig (bet $110 to win $100), one bet per graded game with a market line, no bankroll management or bet sizing. The 95% range comes from the sample size alone (a Wilson interval on the win rate), it does not account for the market line changing between when the bet would have been placed and game time. If the model doesn't beat the market, that should show here as a loss, not get hidden.

The right two columns are always the Test segment (2025 to present), regardless of which segment you've picked above, since that's the only range the model's blend wasn't fit on. A segment-picker result that looks great but disagrees with Test is measuring how well the model fits the years it was tuned on, not whether it predicts new games.
